# Positioning and commercial plan

Copyrail is a proposed independent review desk for small content agencies using several AI writing tools. The narrow entry point is explicit phrase policy: required wording, restricted expressions, and prohibited claims. Reviewers can see exactly why a draft was held.

## Market evidence, checked 2026-09-05

- WRITER markets approved language, writing rules, and brand standards: https://writer.com/brand/
- WRITER marketing workflows include brand and legal review requirements: https://writer.com/solutions/marketing/
- Jasper Brand IQ includes brand guidelines and flags brand violations: https://www.jasper.ai/brand-iq

These primary sources support the existence of the category and strong competition. They do not prove demand for Copyrail or a defensible advantage. Earlier project notes incorrectly characterized WRITER as only a grammar product and included fabricated testimonials. Those claims must not be used.

## Hypothesis to validate

Agencies may pay for a lightweight review layer that works on drafts from any writing tool, with multiple client brands, shareable review reports, an audit trail, and a clear approval workflow. Deterministic checks alone are easy to copy and insufficient differentiation.

## Pricing hypothesis

Starter $49/month; Team $199/month; Desk $499/month. These are proposed prices, not current purchasable plans. At $199/month, 420 paying teams would generate $1,002,960 annual recurring revenue before churn, discounts, tax, and costs. Actual customers and ARR: unverified; no revenue claimed.

## Route to validation

1. Build multi-client workspaces and reviewer workflows with subscriptions.
2. Recruit agency design partners through owner-authorized outreach. No outreach has been sent.
3. Measure draft-review time, retained weekly users, trial-to-paid conversion, and churn.
4. Price against demonstrated saved review hours; target a repeatable buyer segment before scaling acquisition.

A finished app cannot itself establish a $1m valuation or revenue. Commercial success requires customers and operating evidence.
